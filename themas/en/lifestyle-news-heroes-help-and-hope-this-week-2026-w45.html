Lifestyle news: heroes, help and hope this week

November 2, 2026 · BrightNews

This week at BrightNews is dedicated to ordinary people doing extraordinary things for each other. From a family that saved lives after a car crash in Texas, to boxing galas raising money for domestic violence survivors: courage and compassion take centre stage.

Students and neighbours are also showing what cooperation can achieve. A high school in New Jersey collected a quarter million pounds of food for food banks, while students gave a Minneapolis veteran mobility again with a restored car. In North Carolina, a small Halloween gesture grew into a fundraising campaign of more than 2 million dollars for children with cancer.

There is also attention for practical help that makes daily life easier: food banks in New York are expanding their range with pet food, and a new free tool helps young men recognise and prevent boundary-crossing behaviour. Finally, a touching story from England, where a service dog saved the life of the husband of his deaf owner.

Together, these eight stories show how attentiveness, generosity and perseverance make a difference in the lives of others — exactly the kind of news BrightNews stands for.

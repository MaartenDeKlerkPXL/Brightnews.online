Nature Restoration And Green Energy: This Week's Environmental News

September 9, 2026 · BrightNews

This week shows how animals, entrepreneurs and governments are building a greener planet together. From age-old farming tricks to billion-dollar investments in solar energy: smart solutions are emerging everywhere that help nature and climate.

On a wine estate in South Africa, a thousand ducks have been controlling pests for decades without pesticides, while reintroduced beavers in England create water buffers against drought. Smaller animals are also getting attention: a photographer puts chubby mountain hares in the spotlight to promote nature conservation, and the U.S. Coast Guard and the Turtle Hospital in Florida rescue multiple sea turtles, from a heavy adult specimen to 64 vulnerable baby turtles.

On a larger scale, Colombia protects nearly half of its Amazon region against new mining and oil exploration, while innovative building materials and solar energy projects in London and Texas show how sustainability and economy go hand in hand. A Texas coal plant even makes way for a mega solar park. Finally, a roundup of good news brings together remarkable breakthroughs: from bottles that save dolphins to hydrogen trains and more efficient battery recycling.

Together, these stories show that environmental progress often starts with simple, creative ideas that have major impact.
